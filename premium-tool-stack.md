# Night Patrol: Premium Tool Stack (prices checked Oct 9, 2026)

Goal: a console-quality 3D co-op game for iPad and Android tablets with a web-slinging hero in a haunted city.
All prices are USD before tax. Prices come from official pricing pages unless marked *(3rd-party)*. Confirm at checkout because sales change often.

## 1. Engine
| Option | Plan / Price | What it unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Unity Personal | Free (allowed up to $200K revenue + funding) | Full Unity 6 engine with URP and iOS/Android builds |
| Unity Pro | $210/mo per seat, or $2,310/yr prepaid (5% increase on Jan 12, 2026) | Required above $200K. Adds support and extra services. It does **not** improve graphics |
| Unreal Engine 5 | Free. 5% royalty on lifetime gross above $1M per game (Epic Store sales exempt) | Top-tier graphics, but heavy on tablets (large builds, thermal throttling) |
| Three.js | Free (MIT) | Web only. You build everything yourself |
| PlayCanvas | Free (public projects) / Personal $15/mo / Organization $50/seat/mo | Browser editor. Hard to reach a console look on tablet browsers |

**Recommendation: Unity 6 with URP.** It is the most mature mobile pipeline. It works with Synty, Photon Fusion, Mixamo, and the Asset Store, and it gets stable 60fps on an A16 iPad and Snapdragon tablets. Unity Personal is free and legal until you pass $200K. Upgrade to Pro only when you have to.
Sources: https://unity.com/products/pricing-updates · https://unity.com/products/unity-pro · https://www.unrealengine.com/license · https://playcanvas.com/plans

## 6. Multiplayer
| Option | Price | What it unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Photon Fusion | Free 100 CCU (one app) / $95 one-time for 12 mo (+100 CCU, 200 total) / $125/mo for 500 CCU | The best Unity netcode for action co-op, with prediction and lag compensation |
| Unity Netcode + Relay | Free up to 50 avg CCU, then $0.16 per CCU + bandwidth | Native Unity option, but you write more netcode yourself |
| Supabase Realtime | Free tier: 200 concurrent connections, 2M messages/mo *(3rd-party)* | Good for lobbies, presence, and save data. Not suitable for real-time 3D action sync |
Sources: https://www.photonengine.com/fusion/pricing · https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/tools/network/photon-fusion-plus-301825 · https://unity.com/products/gaming-services/pricing · https://supabase.com/pricing
